Smart Madiyan 📚

Smart Madiyan is a free educational app designed especially for students in Kerala who are preparing for teaching examinations such as KTET, LPSA, and UPSA.

I created this project with the goal of making exam preparation easier, more accessible, and more engaging. Studying for competitive exams can sometimes feel boring or repetitive, so Smart Madiyan focuses on simple and convenient practice that students can do directly from their mobile phones.

✨ Features

- 📖 Covers the SCERT syllabus
- 📝 Practice questions for teaching examinations
- 📚 Previous Year Question Papers
- 🏆 Live Study Room to compete and practice with other students
- 📱 Mobile-friendly learning experience
- 🆓 Completely free to use

I strongly believe that practicing with real previous-year question papers can make a significant difference in exam preparation. That is why the project focuses on providing students with useful practice resources in one place.
